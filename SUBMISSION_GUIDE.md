# DecodeX Submission Guide

When your team is ready to submit your solution, ensure you have included all the required deliverables.

## How to Submit
1. Fork this repository.
2. In your fork, create a new folder named `TEAM_[YourTeamName]`.
3. Inside your folder, include the following:
   - `README.md`: Containing your project title, team members, the Problem Statement you chose (Title), and a brief description.
   - `src/`: All the source code for your hardware (Arduino sketches, Python scripts, etc.).
   - `hardware/`: Your circuit diagrams (Fritzing, schematics, or clear photos of hand-drawn diagrams).
   - `video_link.txt`: A URL to your unlisted YouTube/Vimeo demonstration video.
4. Create a Pull Request back to this repository before the deadline.

## Deliverables Checklist
- [ ] Have you chosen a specific Problem Statement?
- [ ] Is your prototype functional?
- [ ] Have you recorded a demonstration video?
- [ ] Is your circuit diagram clear and readable?
- [ ] Have you addressed the specific constraints mentioned in your problem statement?

Submissions missing these elements may not be evaluated.
