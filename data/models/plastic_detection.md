# Smart Plastic Waste Detection and Collection System

## Theme
Environmental & ClimateTech

## Background / Context
Plastic pollution in water bodies (rivers, lakes, drains) causes severe environmental damage and flooding.

## Problem Description
Current methods for identifying and collecting plastic waste in urban water bodies are manual, resource-intensive, and reactive.

## Core Challenge
Develop a system to detect the accumulation of plastic waste in flowing water or drainage systems and either collect it or alert authorities before blockages occur.

## Expected Solution
A surface-floating or drain-mounted monitoring node that detects plastic accumulation using optical sensors or flow-rate changes, coupled with a small-scale collection mechanism or alert system.

## Expected Features
- Essential: Waste accumulation detection, automated alert system, basic local display or indicator.
- Optional: Automated physical collection (e.g., a motorized skimmer arm), solar-powered operation.

## Suggested Hardware
Ultrasonic/Water flow sensors, basic camera/optical sensors, ESP32, simple motors/servos.

## Expected Deliverables
Working prototype, demonstration in a controlled water setup, source code, circuit diagram, brief documentation.

## Constraints
Waterproofing (for the prototype, basic splash resistance is acceptable), affordability.

## Evaluation Criteria
Functionality, technical implementation, feasibility, real-world impact.

## Safety and Ethics
Must not harm local aquatic life. Prototype testing must be done safely in controlled environments.
