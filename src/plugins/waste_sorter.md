# Automated Waste Segregation and Recycling System

## Theme
Environmental & ClimateTech

## Background / Context
Improper waste segregation at the source leads to massive amounts of recyclable materials ending up in landfills.

## Problem Description
Manual sorting of waste is inefficient, hazardous, and often inaccurate. Public bins are frequently contaminated with mixed waste.

## Core Challenge
Design an automated smart bin or sorting mechanism that can classify and segregate waste items into basic categories (e.g., dry/wet, metal/non-metal) at the point of disposal.

## Expected Solution
A prototype bin that uses sensors (inductive, capacitive, or basic optical) to determine the type of waste and automatically routes it to the correct internal compartment.

## Expected Features
- Essential: Waste classification (at least 2 categories), automated routing/sorting mechanism, bin full alert.
- Optional: ML-based image recognition (Raspberry Pi + Camera), usage analytics dashboard.

## Suggested Hardware
Inductive proximity sensors, moisture sensors, servo/stepper motors, Arduino/ESP32, ultrasonic sensors.

## Expected Deliverables
Working prototype, demonstration of sorting, source code, circuit/system diagram, testing results, brief documentation.

## Constraints
Affordability, reliable mechanical sorting, low power consumption.

## Evaluation Criteria
Functionality, accuracy of sorting, technical implementation, real-world impact.

## Safety and Ethics
Must have safety mechanisms to prevent injury from moving parts during operation.
