# PS-11: Open Innovation

## Theme
Open Innovation

## Background / Context
While specific challenges address known issues, the most groundbreaking solutions often come from observing unaddressed problems in local communities, industries, or daily life.

## Problem Description
Many niche, local, or emerging problems lack dedicated technological solutions because they are not yet recognized on a macro scale.

## Core Challenge
Identify a genuine real-world problem that is not covered by the other 10 problem statements, and build a hardware-based solution to address it.

## Expected Solution
An original hardware-based prototype that effectively solves or mitigates the identified problem. Purely software projects are not eligible.

## Eligible Themes
Any real-world sector (e.g., Healthcare, Agriculture, Accessibility, Smart Cities, Transportation, Manufacturing).

## Expected Features
- Essential: A clear problem definition, functional hardware prototype, measurable impact or demonstration.
- Optional: Cloud integration, advanced analytics, custom PCB design.

## Suggested Hardware
Open to any affordable, commonly available components (e.g., ESP32, Arduino, Raspberry Pi, appropriate sensors/actuators).

## Expected Deliverables
Working prototype, demonstration, source code, circuit/system diagram, testing results, and brief documentation.

## Constraints
Affordability, portability, reliability, usability, and practical limitations. Do not use extremely hazardous materials or rare components.

## Evaluation Criteria
Functionality, technical implementation, innovation, feasibility, usability, and real-world impact. The severity of the identified problem will also be judged.

## Safety and Ethics
Must adhere to all standard safety regulations. Prototypes must not pose a risk of fire, shock, or injury. Focus on user consent and privacy if collecting data.
