# DecodeX Hackathon - Participant Rules

Welcome to **DecodeX**! Please read the following rules carefully before beginning.

## Eligibility
- All team members must be enrolled in a recognized university or college.
- Teams can have between 2 to 4 members.

## General Rules
- **No Sabotage**: Do not attempt to modify, delete, or overwrite the repository contents to hide clues from other teams.
- **Fair Play**: Do not share the problem statements publicly on social media or with other teams.
- **Open Innovation**: You are allowed to work on the openly available Open Innovation track, or you may hunt for the 10 hidden hardware problem statements within this repository.

## Safety & Ethics
- **Safety First**: Your hardware prototypes must be safe. Do not use high-voltage mains electricity without proper isolation. Do not use hazardous chemicals.
- **Ethics**: If your project involves medical or surveillance concepts, strictly follow the ethical constraints listed in your chosen problem statement. Your prototype is a demonstration, not a production-ready medical or security device.

## Submission Requirements
1. A working hardware prototype.
2. Source code used in your prototype.
3. A circuit diagram or system architecture diagram.
4. A 2-3 minute demonstration video.
5. A brief documentation report (PDF).
